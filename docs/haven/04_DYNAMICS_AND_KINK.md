### Archetype 1: Power Exchange & Dominance / Submission (D/s)

Power exchange is the intentional, consensual transfer of authority, decision-making, or physical control from one partner (Submissive, Slave, Bottom, Pet) to another (Dominant, Master, Mistress, Owner).

- **Key Sub-Types:**
  - *Consensual 24/7 Power Exchange (TPE / Master-Slave):* Continuous protocol governing daily habits, posture, attire, language, and service.
  - *Contextual / Scene-Based D/s:* Authority exists only during scheduled play sessions or specific ritual hours.
  - *Female Dominance (Femdom):* Intentional centering of female authority, worship, financial domination (findom), or chastity control.
  - *Service & Devotion:* Focus on acts of service, domestic excellence, personal assistance, and emotional care.
- **Dedicated Mechanics:**
  - **Protocols & Postures:** Daily adherence checks (e.g., eye contact rules, greeting protocols, kneeling timers).
  - **Task & Chore Engine:** Dominant-assigned tasks with mandatory completion windows and proof attachments.
  - **Rewards & Consensual Penalties:** Points, privileges, or erotic forfeit decks (e.g., writing lines, extra chores, impact scenes) agreed upon prior to execution.
  - **The Submissive's Veto:** Strict preservation of the submissive’s agency via safewords and the right to call a formal conference at any time.

### Archetype 2: Chastity & Orgasm Control

Chastity involves the intentional physical or psychological restriction of male or female sexual release, typically managed through physical cages, biometric smart locks, or strict psychological denial protocols.

- **Key Roles:**
  - *Keyholder (Controller):* Holds physical keys, digital tokens, or authorization codes; determines release schedules, hygiene sessions, and pleasure allowances.
  - *Chaste (Wearer / Controlled):* Wears a chastity device or adheres to an honor-based denial agreement; submits to inspection, edging, or denial.
- **Dedicated Mechanics:**
  - **Lockout Timers & Countdowns:** Real-time visibility into the duration of current lockup, longest continuous lockup streak, and upcoming release windows.
  - **Hygiene & Inspection Windows:** Automated safety prompts requiring cage removal for cleaning, skin inspection, and physical health checks every 24–48 hours.
  - **Emergency Release Code:** A sovereign, tamper-evident emergency release workflow allowing the chaste partner to unlock immediately in case of pain, edema, medical need, or travel without waiting for keyholder response.
  - **Orgasm Categorization:** Granular logging of sexual outcomes:
    - *Full / Granted Release:* Keyholder-approved climax.
    - *Ruined Orgasm:* Climax induced without erection or permitted pleasure, neutralizing physical tension without psychological reward.
    - *Edging Count:* Tracked counts of brought-to-the-brink arousal states followed by mandatory cooldown.
    - *Denial:* High arousal terminated with immediate lockup.

### Archetype 3: Consensual Non-Monogamy & Cuckold / Hotwife

Dynamics in which one or more partners engage in sexual, romantic, or dating relationships with outside individuals, practiced with complete transparency, enthusiastic consent, and specific erotic rituals.

## 2. Practice Stages vs. Consent Stages

One of Haven's core architectural innovations is the strict mathematical and conceptual separation between **What an adult is interested in** and **What an adult has consented to do right now**.

```
                THE PRACTICE PROGRESSION PIPELINE

[ INTERESTED / FANTASY ]  ──► Private curiosity or shared fantasy.
          │                   NO consent to execute.
          ▼
    [ EXPLORING ]         ──► Open dialogue, reading, boundary calibration.
          │                   Safe testing of edge conditions.
          ▼
      [ AGREED ]          ──► Formal covenant negotiated and signed.
          │                   Terms, review dates, and limits fixed.
          ▼
      [ ACTIVE ]          ──► Operational state. Can be initiated in sessions,
          │                   tasks, or rituals with real-time readiness.
          ▼
      [ PAUSED ]          ──► Temporarily deactivated due to life, stress,
          │                   or fatigue. History intact.
          ▼
  [ HARD BOUNDARY ]       ──► Inviolable veto. Software refuses prompts,
                              tasks, or execution suggestions.
```

### State Definitions

| Stage | Meaning | Software Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `interested` | The user finds the kink intriguing or experiences sexual arousal thinking about it. | Visible in private wishlists or double-blind discovery decks. **Never** automatically triggers a task or session prompt. |
| `exploring` | Partners have mutually agreed to discuss, research, or lightly sample the practice. | Haven surfaces educational tips, boundary checklists, and sample negotiation questions. |
| `agreed` | Both partners have formally negotiated terms, safety rules, and limits within an Agreement. | Available in the dynamic’s active toolbox; ready to be scheduled into sessions or rituals. |
| `active` | Currently scheduled, assigned, or live in practice. | Actively generates tasks, reminders, timers, and session templates. Requires real-time participant readiness before any scene. |
| `paused` | Temporarily suspended by either partner. | All automated timers and notifications for this practice are silenced. Reactivation requires mutual confirmation. |
| `hard_boundary` | An absolute, permanent or non-negotiable limit. | Completely suppressed from all generative prompts, suggestions, and task generators. |

## 3. Boundary Nuance: Soft Limits, Hard Limits & Triggers

To prevent misunderstanding, Haven allows users to annotate boundaries with three granular tiers:

1. **Hard Limit (Absolute Veto):**
   - *Definition:* Under zero circumstances will this activity occur.
   - *System Enforcement:* Cannot be overridden by any keyholder or dominant role. The app will never present tasks, suggestions, or games containing this tag.
2. **Soft Limit (Conditional Caution):**
   - *Definition:* An activity that requires extreme caution, specific physical/emotional prerequisites, slow introduction, or constant verbal check-ins.
   - *System Enforcement:* When a session includes a soft limit, the pre-scene readiness check requires explicit verbal confirmation and highlights the specific conditional notes.
3. **Trigger / Trauma Notice:**
   - *Definition:* Specific words, physical touches, or psychological scenarios associated with past non-consensual trauma.
   - *System Enforcement:* Permanently surfaced in the dynamic's Safety Plan; clearly visible to any partner entering a play session.
