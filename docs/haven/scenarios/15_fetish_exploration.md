### Scenario 1: The First Latex Dressing & Shimmer Ritual
- **Context:** Couple purchasing their first made-to-measure latex catsuit and dress.
- **Dynamic:** Material fetish immersion and aesthetic reverence.
- **What they want:** Experiencing the tight, second-skin compression, smell, and shine of pure natural rubber.
- **Before:** Gathering latex dressing aids (talc or silicone dressing aid; polishing spray; ensuring zero petroleum products touch the rubber).
- **Experience:** The partner uses silicone lube to slide smoothly into the garment without tearing. The other partner meticulously applies silicone shine spray with a micro-fiber cloth until the rubber gleams like obsidian.
- **Branches:** If heat exhaustion or sweating causes overheating, the wearer steps into a cool room and unzips immediately.
- **Aftercare:** Undressing carefully, warm shower with mild soap to remove silicone aid, washing the latex garment in gentle rubber wash, and hanging it to dry.
- **Reflection:** Both are captivated by the hypnotic tactile transformation that latex creates.
- **Haven opportunities:** Latex care, cleaning, and storage guide; overheating safety timer; material maintenance reminders.
