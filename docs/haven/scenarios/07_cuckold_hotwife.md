### Scenario 7: Managing Jealousy Spikes & Boundary Rupture Repair
- **Context:** Couple where a date ran 2 hours late without a check-in text, triggering intense marital panic.
- **Dynamic:** Crisis management and boundary restoration in non-monogamy.
- **What they want:** De-escalating acute panic, processing the boundary breach honestly, and repairing trust without blowing up the relationship.
- **Before:** The agreed rule was a text by 11:00 PM; her phone battery died and she didn't text until 1:00 AM.
- **Experience:** Upon her return, emotions are high. Following Haven's 'Crisis De-escalation Protocol', they agree to NO late-night shouting. They sit on the sofa, drink water, verify physical safety, and postpone the post-mortem until 10:00 AM after sleep.
- **Branches:** Next morning, they review what happened calmly. She apologizes sincerely for failing to monitor her battery; he shares how terrified he felt thinking she had been in an accident.
- **Aftercare:** They institute a portable battery pack rule for future dates and place all outside dating on a 60-day pause to reconnect.
- **Reflection:** Realizing that mistakes will happen, and that how a couple repairs after a breach defines their longevity.
- **Haven opportunities:** Boundary rupture repair workflow; cooling-off delay timer for arguments; dynamic pause button.
