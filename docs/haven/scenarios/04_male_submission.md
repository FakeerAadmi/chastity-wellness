### Scenario 1: The Burden Release — Complete Abdication of Decisions
- **Context:** Male corporate founder overwhelmed by decision fatigue at work, living with an intuitive female partner.
- **Dynamic:** Consensual leadership transfer and protective dominance.
- **What they want:** For 48 hours, he makes zero decisions: what to eat, when to shower, what to wear, and when to sleep.
- **Before:** He signs off work, places his phone in a lockbox, and verbally hands sovereignty over to his partner on Friday evening.
- **Experience:** She hands him clothes, prepares balanced meals, tells him when to take a warm bath, and guides his physical rest. He experiences a profound drop in cortisol and mental racing.
- **Branches:** If an urgent work crisis occurs, the agreement specifies that only she monitors his phone and decides whether it merits breaking his retreat.
- **Aftercare:** Sunday evening re-entry conversation over soup, gradually restoring his autonomy for Monday morning.
- **Reflection:** He realizes how much chronic stress he carries and how deeply he trusts her protective strength.
- **Haven opportunities:** Decision abdication schedule builder; emergency contact delegation rules; re-entry gradual unlock sequence.
