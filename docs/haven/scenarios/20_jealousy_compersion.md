### Scenario 1: Deconstructing the Jealousy Spiral — The 5 Whys Technique
- **Context:** A partner feels a sudden, overwhelming wave of burning jealousy when seeing their spouse receive a flirty text from a date.
- **Dynamic:** Cognitive and emotional inquiry into jealousy roots.
- **What they want:** Interrogating the burning feeling before reacting with anger, accusations, or passive aggression.
- **Before:** Opening Haven's 'Jealousy Deconstruction Protocol' and finding a quiet room with a journal.
- **Experience:** Using the '5 Whys', the partner digs deeper: 1. I am jealous because he texted her. 2. Because she is pretty. 3. Because I fear he will find her more exciting. 4. Because I feel insecure about my post-baby body. 5. Root: I am terrified of being abandoned.
- **Branches:** Having identified the root fear (abandonment), the partner approaches the spouse with vulnerability ('I felt a spike of fear about our connection') rather than anger ('Why are you texting her?!').
- **Aftercare:** The spouse hugs them tightly, offering deep verbal reassurance of lifelong commitment and physical adoration.
- **Reflection:** Jealousy is rarely about the third party; it is almost always a smoke alarm alerting to an internal tender wound.
- **Haven opportunities:** 5 Whys jealousy worksheet; emotional root-cause identifier; vulnerable communication prompts.

### Scenario 2: The First Glimmer of Compersion — Cultivating Vicarious Joy
- **Context:** A partner whose spouse has returned from a magical, romantic weekend date with a lover.
- **Dynamic:** Cultivating compersion (sympathetic joy in a partner's happiness).
- **What they want:** Experiencing genuine happiness for the partner's glowing fulfillment without feeling diminished.
- **Before:** Setting an intention to listen with an open, loving heart and looking for the light in the partner's eyes.
- **Experience:** As the spouse shares photos of the art museum and shares how refreshed she feels, the partner feels a warm, unexpected bloom of love: 'Seeing you so happy makes me happy. You deserve this joy.'
- **Branches:** If a brief twinge of envy surfaces, they notice it without judgment and let it pass like a cloud across the sun.
- **Aftercare:** Making dinner together, enjoying the radiant energy that the partner brought back into their shared home.
- **Reflection:** Compersion is like watching your child or best friend win an award: their victory does not take away from yours.
- **Haven opportunities:** Compersion journal prompts; joy sharing check-in; positive vicarious emotion tracking.

### Scenario 3: Somatic Soothing for the Physical Jealousy Panic Attack
- **Context:** A partner at home experiencing acute physiological fight-or-flight symptoms (racing heart, nausea, cold sweat) while partner is on a date.
- **Dynamic:** Nervous system regulation during acute jealousy episodes.
- **What they want:** Calming the physiological emergency response through somatic grounding rather than cognitive spiraling.
- **Before:** Recognizing that the primitive reptilian brain interprets romantic jealousy as a life-or-death tribal exile threat.
- **Experience:** The partner activates Haven's 'Somatic Reset': splash freezing water on the face (mammalian dive reflex), 4-7-8 breathing, putting on a heavy weighted blanket, and progressive muscle clenching and release.
- **Branches:** Within 15 minutes, heart rate drops from 120 bpm to 72 bpm. The mental catastrophe subsides as the nervous system feels physically safe.
- **Aftercare:** Drinking warm peppermint tea, watching a comforting childhood movie, and resting until the partner arrives home.
- **Reflection:** You cannot think your way out of a nervous system panic; you must soothe the body first before the mind can listen.
- **Haven opportunities:** Emergency somatic panic reset audio guide; mammalian dive reflex instructions; heart-rate breathing sync.

### Scenario 4: Comparison & Envy — 'They Have Something I Don't'
- **Context:** A partner comparing themselves unfavorably to a partner's new lover (wealthier, younger, or more adventurous).
- **Dynamic:** Dismantling toxic comparison and reclaiming unique value.
- **What they want:** Stopping the toxic mental loop of comparing personal flaws against a new person's highlight reel.
- **Before:** Reviewing Haven's 'Uniqueness Inventory': listing the 5 unique, irreplaceable pillars they bring to the marriage.
- **Experience:** The couple sits down for an honest calibration. The partner shares their feelings of inadequacy. The other partner lovingly dismantles the illusion: 'I don't love you for the things they have; I love you for our history, your kindness, your humor, and our shared life.'
- **Branches:** They agree to avoid sharing granular details about the lover's wealth or lifestyle that trigger comparison wounds.
- **Aftercare:** Revisiting old photo albums of their own adventures, reaffirming the deep roots of their bond.
- **Reflection:** A garden has roses and lilies; the beauty of the rose does not make the lily defective.
- **Haven opportunities:** Uniqueness inventory worksheet; comparison trigger filter settings; partner value affirmation cards.

### Scenario 5: The Fear of Replacement — The Core Poly Anxiety
- **Context:** A long-term nesting partner terrified that the new partner will eventually replace them entirely.
- **Dynamic:** Securing relational attachment and primary security.
- **What they want:** Irrevocable, concrete evidence that new love does not subtract from existing love.
- **Before:** Reading literature on secure attachment in non-monogamy.
- **Experience:** The couple drafts an explicit 'Core Relational Anchor Covenant' detailing all the non-negotiable aspects of their partnership (nesting, legal marriage, finances, shared retirement) that are not on the table for outside partners.
- **Branches:** When moments of insecurity arise in the future, they re-read this covenant together as an emotional anchor.
- **Aftercare:** A dedicated weekend trip just the two of them, reaffirming their shared future dreams.
- **Reflection:** Explicit structural clarity banishes the phantom fear of replacement.
- **Haven opportunities:** Core relational anchor covenant builder; non-negotiable life pillars checklist; security affirmation log.

### Scenario 6: Jealousy as an Erotic Turn-On (Hotwife / Cuckold Reclamation)
- **Context:** A partner who discovers that their jealousy has an erotic, intensely arousing component.
- **Dynamic:** Channeling jealousy into sexual passion and erotic submission.
- **What they want:** Alchemizing painful jealousy into intense sexual desire and passionate reclamation.
- **Before:** Understanding the psychological link between perceived threat, territorial instincts, and male sexual arousal.
- **Experience:** Instead of suppressing the jealousy, the partner leans into the feeling, using the heightened heart rate and adrenaline to fuel deep, wild passion during the wife's return. The lovemaking is more electric than ever.
- **Branches:** If the feeling crosses from erotic excitement into genuine humiliation or misery, they drop the erotic frame immediately for emotional holding.
- **Aftercare:** Holding each other in the dark, whispering honest admissions of love and gratitude.
- **Reflection:** Recognizing that human emotions are multifaceted: jealousy can be an enemy or an erotic fuel when handled with absolute trust.
- **Haven opportunities:** Jealousy alchemy self-test; arousal vs. distress calibration slider; safe reclamation guide.

### Scenario 7: The Metamour Celebration Gift — Active Compersion in Practice
- **Context:** A partner preparing a birthday gift or travel basket for their metamour.
- **Dynamic:** Intentional generosity and building kitchen-table warmth.
- **What they want:** Expressing active goodwill toward the person who brings joy to their shared partner.
- **Before:** Consulting the shared hinge partner on the metamour's favorite snacks, tea, or books.
- **Experience:** The partner packs a thoughtful care package with home-baked cookies and a kind note: 'Happy birthday Alex, thanks for being such a wonderful person in our world.'
- **Branches:** The metamour is deeply moved and sends a heartfelt thank-you text, solidifying a warm friendship.
- **Aftercare:** The household enjoys an evening of harmony and shared laughter.
- **Reflection:** Generosity is the ultimate antidote to petty territorial jealousy.
- **Haven opportunities:** Metamour gift idea generator; polycule event celebration reminders; goodwill tracking.
