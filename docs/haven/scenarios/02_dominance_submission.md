### Scenario 1: The First Formal Collaring Ceremony
- **Context:** Couple practicing D/s for 1 year deciding to formalize their commitment with a silver collar.
- **Dynamic:** Master / slave or Dominant / submissive relationship contract.
- **What they want:** A momentous, sacred ceremony marking the transition from casual kink to dedicated power exchange.
- **Before:** Both draft and sign their mutual agreements on Haven, detailing the scope of authority, duties, and the Dominant's obligations of care.
- **Experience:** In candlelight, Submissive kneels and presents the collar. Dominant reads the covenants, places the collar around their neck, and locks it, sealing the commitment.
- **Branches:** If emotional vulnerability brings intense tears of overwhelm, Dominant pauses to hold Submissive before completing the vows.
- **Aftercare:** Shared meal, quiet champagne toast, looking at photos of their journey together.
- **Reflection:** Collaring is recognized not as ownership of a person, but as an agreement of stewardship and mutual trust.
- **Haven opportunities:** Covenant document generation with printable PDF; collaring anniversary reminders; bilateral signature vault.

### Scenario 2: Morning Devotion & Posture Protocol
- **Context:** Co-habitating partners where the submissive prepares morning coffee under formal protocol.
- **Dynamic:** Daily living protocol integrated with domestic life.
- **What they want:** To instill quiet reverence and erotic mindfulness into early morning routines.
- **Before:** Protocol agreement: eyes downcast until acknowledged, two hands on the tray, kneeling to present the cup.
- **Experience:** Submissive wakes 20 minutes earlier, prepares the coffee precisely to specification, enters the study, kneels, and waits quietly until the Dominant looks up and gives permission to rise.
- **Branches:** If work schedules require rushing, Dominant issues a brief 'protocol waiver' so the submissive does not feel conflicted or failing.
- **Aftercare:** Dominant offers a gentle touch on the cheek and warm verbal gratitude before morning routines begin.
- **Reflection:** Submissive feels centered and relieved of morning anxiety through structured service.
- **Haven opportunities:** Protocol waiver quick-action toggle; morning protocol step checklist; daily devotion streak counter without penalties.

### Scenario 3: Wardrobe Selection & Dressing Dictation
- **Context:** Professional couple where the Dominant curates the submissive's daily wardrobe.
- **Dynamic:** Aesthetic control and psychological surrender.
- **What they want:** Erotic anticipation of wearing attire chosen entirely by the partner.
- **Before:** Dominant inspects weather and calendar schedule on Haven to ensure attire is business-appropriate and comfortable.
- **Experience:** Dominant lays out undergarments, suit, shoes, and jewelry. Submissive dresses silently in front of a full-length mirror under the Dominant's watchful eye.
- **Branches:** If an item does not fit or conflicts with a specific meeting requirement, Submissive politely requests an adjustment under protocol.
- **Aftercare:** A slow kiss and mutual confirmation of pride in how the submissive presents to the world.
- **Reflection:** Submissive notices carrying a feeling of being 'kept and cherished' throughout their workday.
- **Haven opportunities:** Daily wardrobe request task; photo submission for approval; calendar context integration.

### Scenario 4: Evening Kneeling Debrief & Accountability Report
- **Context:** High-powered couple using a 15-minute evening check-in to clear mental baggage.
- **Dynamic:** Strict accountability and mental inventory submission.
- **What they want:** Transparent confession of daily stresses, slip-ups, and dietary/exercise goals without defensive arguing.
- **Before:** Agreement that during the kneeling report, the Dominant listens without interrupting until the submissive completes their account.
- **Experience:** Submissive kneels beside the Dominant's chair, reporting on three completed tasks, one struggle with anxiety, and their water intake. Dominant provides calm feedback and reassurance.
- **Branches:** If submissive had a severe emotional breakdown at work, Dominant immediately suspends protocol and pulls them into their lap for comfort.
- **Aftercare:** Holding each other in bed, transitioning from formal structure to intimate tenderness.
- **Reflection:** Both find that formal structure prevents mundane discussions from turning into marital bickering.
- **Haven opportunities:** Evening debrief prompt template; task progress summary card; protocol-to-comfort transition guide.

### Scenario 5: Consensual Financial Ledger & Budget Submission
- **Context:** Partners managing shared finances where submissive submits all personal expenditures for approval.
- **Dynamic:** Consensual financial discipline and lifestyle power exchange.
- **What they want:** Elimination of impulse spending and reinforcing financial alignment.
- **Before:** Establishing strict discretionary spending thresholds ($50 daily allowance; all larger items submitted 24 hours prior).
- **Experience:** Submissive logs personal purchases into a shared tracker. Dominant reviews weekly, offering praise for savings and disciplined corrections for impulsive leaks.
- **Branches:** If an emergency purchase is required (e.g., car battery), emergency waiver rules allow instant purchase with retroactive notification.
- **Aftercare:** Celebrating achieving a shared vacation savings goal with an affectionate evening out.
- **Reflection:** Relieving the submissive of financial anxiety while building shared relationship wealth.
- **Haven opportunities:** Discretionary allowance ledger; purchase permission requests; emergency spend waiver flag.

### Scenario 6: Silence Protocol & Auditory Surrender
- **Context:** Couple practicing sensory power exchange over a weekend getaway.
- **Dynamic:** Consensual enforced silence (Vow of Silence).
- **What they want:** Deepening non-verbal awareness and quieting mental chatter.
- **Before:** Equipping submissive with a discreet notepad or gesture card deck for essential communication.
- **Experience:** For 24 hours in a secluded cabin, the submissive may not speak a single word. Communication is strictly through eye contact, breath, and physical compliance.
- **Branches:** If safety, nausea, or urgent physical needs arise, the submissive taps the yellow card to speak freely without penalty.
- **Aftercare:** At the conclusion of the 24 hours, the Dominant asks a single question ('What was your most profound thought?') and listens quietly.
- **Reflection:** The submissive notes that silence sharpened their somatic and emotional presence ten-fold.
- **Haven opportunities:** Digital flashcards for silent protocol; silence timer with non-verbal emergency buttons; journal prompts for post-silence.

### Scenario 7: The Periodic Power Review & Contract Renegotiation
- **Context:** D/s partnership conducting their quarterly relationship calibration.
- **Dynamic:** Meta-level governance outside the dynamic (Equal adult negotiations).
- **What they want:** To inspect the health, sustainability, and joy of the power exchange without fear of disappointing the other.
- **Before:** Both complete independent Haven self-assessments rating energy, safety, sexual fulfillment, and protocol fatigue.
- **Experience:** Sitting as equals at a neutral dining table with tea and laptops. They review each agreement clause by clause, celebrating what worked and modifying clauses that caused friction.
- **Branches:** If one partner expresses burnout, the affected dynamic is placed on a temporary 30-day pause.
- **Aftercare:** A celebratory dinner as equal romantic partners.
- **Reflection:** Recognizing that robust negotiation is the foundation that allows deep surrender to exist safely.
- **Haven opportunities:** Quarterly agreement review wizard; comparative rating delta graphs; clause-by-clause amendment tracker.
