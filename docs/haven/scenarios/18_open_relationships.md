### Scenario 1: Opening the Marriage — The 6-Month Exploration Pact
- **Context:** Monogamous couple of 10 years deciding to transition into an open relationship structure.
- **Dynamic:** Ethical Non-Monogamy (ENM) foundational transition.
- **What they want:** Exploring sexual variety with outside partners while keeping their marriage as the central, unshakeable priority.
- **Before:** Spending 6 months reading literature, attending couples therapy, and codifying their boundaries on Haven before downloading dating apps.
- **Experience:** They sign their 'Haven Open Relationship Charter': barrier protection for all outside sex, quarterly STI testing, no dating mutual friends or colleagues, and a strict rule that either partner can call for a temporary pause at any time.
- **Branches:** They agree to a 90-day trial period where they only flirt or go for drinks, with zero sexual contact, to observe emotional reactions first.
- **Aftercare:** A celebratory weekend getaway affirming their deep love and excitement for their evolving journey.
- **Reflection:** Taking months to build the foundation prevents catastrophic misunderstandings later.
- **Haven opportunities:** ENM charter template; boundary checklist builder; scheduled 90-day trial review milestones.

### Scenario 2: The First Outside Date — Managing the Waiting Partner's Anxiety
- **Context:** One partner goes on their very first dinner date with someone met on a dating app.
- **Dynamic:** Primary partner emotional containment and compersion cultivation.
- **What they want:** The dating partner enjoys their evening, while the waiting partner practices emotional self-soothing without sabotaging the date.
- **Before:** Agreeing on logistics: date ends by 11:00 PM; a single check-in text when leaving the restaurant; waiting partner plans a fun evening with friends.
- **Experience:** While she is out at dinner, he hosts a movie night with his best friend. When 10:45 PM arrives, her text chimes: 'Date was nice, heading home now!' His anxiety transforms into relief and pride.
- **Branches:** If the waiting partner feels an acute panic attack, they use Haven's grounding exercises rather than bombarding the date with frantic texts.
- **Aftercare:** Warm reunion at the door, a cup of tea, sharing the funny stories from the date, and falling asleep together.
- **Reflection:** The waiting partner learns that anxiety peaks in anticipation and dissolves with communication.
- **Haven opportunities:** Waiting partner anxiety toolkit and distraction planner; curfew check-in notification; date completion log.

### Scenario 3: Quarterly STI Testing & Health Passport Exchange
- **Context:** Open couple managing sexual health diligently across multiple casual partners.
- **Dynamic:** Sexual health stewardship and transparent screening.
- **What they want:** Uncompromised physical safety through regular, documented clinical testing.
- **Before:** Setting recurring quarterly calendar alarms on Haven for comprehensive STI panels (HIV, Syphilis, Gonorrhea, Chlamydia, Hep C).
- **Experience:** Both visit the clinic, receive digital lab results, and upload them to their private Haven Health Passport. They review the clean results over breakfast and update their partner profile badges.
- **Branches:** If a positive result for a treatable infection occurs, immediate full disclosure to all partners, immediate medical treatment, and complete sexual pause until clear.
- **Aftercare:** Mutual appreciation for taking sexual health seriously as an act of love.
- **Reflection:** Recognizing that regular testing removes fear and enables confident, joyful intimacy.
- **Haven opportunities:** Encrypted STI lab result vault; quarterly screening reminder alerts; partner health passport badge.

### Scenario 4: The Calendar Balancing Act — Prioritizing the Primary Relationship
- **Context:** Busy open couple feeling stretched thin between work, outside dates, and marital time.
- **Dynamic:** Time management and primary relationship defense.
- **What they want:** Ensuring that outside dating does not cannibalize their dedicated marital quality time.
- **Before:** Establishing the 'Haven 2:1 Rule': for every 1 outside date night, they must schedule 2 dedicated, distraction-free date nights together.
- **Experience:** They sit down on Sunday night with their shared calendar, blocking out Friday night as 'Marital Sanctuary Time' before any outside dates are permitted to be scheduled.
- **Branches:** If one partner feels neglected, they can place a 14-day hold on outside dating to recharge primary connection.
- **Aftercare:** Enjoying their protected date night with phones off, deeply engaged in conversation.
- **Reflection:** They realize that non-monogamy requires exceptional calendar discipline and intentionality.
- **Haven opportunities:** Shared relationship calendar; 2:1 primary-to-secondary ratio tracker; date-night protection locks.

### Scenario 5: Navigating 'NRE' (New Relationship Energy) Overwhelm
- **Context:** One partner falls into intense, intoxicating New Relationship Energy with a new secondary lover.
- **Dynamic:** Managing NRE without neglecting the long-term partner.
- **What they want:** Enjoying the chemical high of NRE while actively grounding and reassuring the primary partner.
- **Before:** Reviewing Haven's NRE guide: recognizing dopamine spikes, idealization, and the risk of taking the primary for granted.
- **Experience:** The partner experiencing NRE consciously channels that surplus energy back into their marriage—bringing flowers, initiating passionate dates, and verbally expressing gratitude to the primary partner.
- **Branches:** If the primary partner feels like an annoying roommate in comparison to the shiny new lover, they call an 'NRE Calibration Summit' to address the imbalance gently.
- **Aftercare:** Deep listening, validating the primary partner's feelings, and scaling back secondary text frequency during shared home time.
- **Reflection:** NRE is like a high-powered drug: beautiful when channeled, destructive when unmonitored.
- **Haven opportunities:** NRE self-assessment scale; partner appreciation reminder prompts; secondary messaging curfew settings.

### Scenario 6: Handling Unplanned Feelings & Polyamory Evolution
- **Context:** A casual 'friends with benefits' connection unexpectedly develops into deep romantic love.
- **Dynamic:** Transitioning from open/casual sex to polyamory.
- **What they want:** Honest disclosure of emotional evolution without secrecy or deceit.
- **Before:** The original agreement was strictly casual sex; romantic feelings were supposed to be avoided.
- **Experience:** Rather than hiding the feelings in shame, the partner comes forward immediately: 'I am noticing real emotional attachment developing with Sarah, and I want to talk about what that means for us.'
- **Branches:** They explore whether their structure can accommodate polyamory or if the secondary connection needs to be de-escalated to preserve the marriage.
- **Aftercare:** Crying together, holding space for uncertainty, and deciding to see a poly-informed therapist together.
- **Reflection:** Realizing that feelings cannot always be controlled by contract, but honesty can always be maintained.
- **Haven opportunities:** Relationship transition discussion framework; polyamory readiness assessment; therapy preparation notes.

### Scenario 7: Calling the Pause Button — Relationship First
- **Context:** Couple facing major life stress (job loss, family illness) needing to close ranks.
- **Dynamic:** Exercising the Emergency Monogamy / Pause clause.
- **What they want:** Temporarily closing the relationship to outside dating to focus 100% of their energy on each other.
- **Before:** The foundational agreement explicitly included: 'Either partner can activate a 60-day pause without defense or argument.'
- **Experience:** One partner activates the Pause on Haven. Both respectfully notify their secondary connections that they are taking a hiatus for personal family reasons, stepping back into focused couple time.
- **Branches:** Secondary partners who are ethical and mature respect the boundary; any secondary who creates drama is permanently retired.
- **Aftercare:** A deep sigh of relief, cooking dinner together, and facing the outside life crisis as an unbreakable team.
- **Reflection:** Knowing the Pause button actually works gives both partners the ultimate psychological safety in non-monogamy.
- **Haven opportunities:** One-click 'Dynamic Pause' status toggle; polite secondary notification templates; pause-period reconnection journal.
