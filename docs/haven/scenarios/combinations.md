### Scenario 1: Femdom + Chastity + Cuckoldry (The Triad of Surrender)
- **Context:** Married couple of 10 years where the wife holds complete sexual sovereignty, the husband is long-term chaste, and she dates secondary lovers.
- **Dynamic:** Interlocking power exchange: Keyholding Hotwife / Chaste Submissive Cuckold.
- **What they want:** A harmonious life where his sexual energy is channeled into domestic service and worship of her outside romantic fulfillment.
- **Before:** Comprehensive agreements on Haven: barrier protection for all outside encounters; weekly hygiene unlock inspection; 10:30 PM date-night check-in.

- **Branches:** If outside dating creates unexpected anxiety, a scheduled 30-day dynamic pause allows the couple to focus inward.
- **Aftercare:** Warm cuddles, verbal affirmations of his central role in her life, and shared morning coffee.
- **Reflection:** Combining these three dynamics creates an ecosystem of profound mutual satisfaction when anchored in radical transparency.
- **Haven opportunities:** Multi-dynamic dashboard linking Chastity + Hotwife modules; combined date log and lock status; compersion reflection prompts.
