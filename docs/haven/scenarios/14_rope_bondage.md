### Scenario 1: The First Single-Column Tie & Safety Shears Drill
- **Context:** Beginner couple wanting to learn rope bondage safely from home.
- **Dynamic:** Rigger / Model foundational education.
- **What they want:** Mastering the basic single-column tie on the wrist without pinching or nerve compression.
- **Before:** Mandatory safety drill: holding EMT shears in hand and practicing cutting rope off in under 5 seconds.
- **Experience:** Using conditioned jute rope, the Rigger wraps the wrist with even tension, tucking the bights neatly. The Model checks mobility and verifies that fingers are warm and pink.
- **Branches:** If the rope slips into the wrist crease or causes tingling, it is untied immediately and repositioned 2 inches up the forearm.
- **Aftercare:** Rope removal, rubbing the wrists with sweet almond oil, and practicing tying the knot together.
- **Reflection:** Learning that rope safety starts with muscle memory and emergency preparedness.
- **Haven opportunities:** Emergency shears drill confirmation; single-column tie video guide; nerve safety anatomical map.
