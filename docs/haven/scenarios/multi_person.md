### Scenario 1: The V-Dynamic Birthday Celebration — Metamour Harmony
- **Context:** A hinge partner celebrating her birthday with her nesting husband and her secondary partner.
- **Dynamic:** V-structure with healthy kitchen-table compersion.
- **What they want:** A harmonious birthday dinner where both men celebrate her without competition or jealousy.
- **Before:** The two metamours collaborate secretly on Haven to split the costs and plan the surprise dinner.
- **Experience:** At the restaurant, the husband sits to her left, the secondary partner to her right. The atmosphere is warm, respectful, and joyful. They toast her kindness, intelligence, and the life they all share.
- **Branches:** If outside diners give curious looks, the trio smiles warmly, unbothered by outside convention.
- **Aftercare:** Returning to the house for cake; the secondary partner heads home at 10 PM with warm hugs, while the husband stays to tuck her in.
- **Reflection:** Metamour cooperation creates an abundance of love that enriches everyone involved.
- **Haven opportunities:** Multi-partner event collaboration space; shared gift coordination board; seating etiquette notes.

### Scenario 2: The Triad Bedroom Choreography & Rotation Ritual
- **Context:** Three committed partners in an equilateral triad navigating fair physical intimacy.
- **Dynamic:** Triadic physical intimacy and rotation.
- **What they want:** Ensuring no partner feels like an awkward 'third wheel' during shared sexual intimacy.
- **Before:** Reviewing their bedroom agreement: centering one partner at a time, moving in slow waves, keeping eyes open.
- **Experience:** On the king-sized bed, two partners focus all their sensory attention, massage, and kisses on the third partner until they are glowing. Then the focus rotates gracefully to the next partner.
- **Branches:** If one partner feels sexually overwhelmed, they can tap out into 'Cuddle Mode' in the center of the bed while the others continue.
- **Aftercare:** Lying intertwined like puppies, drinking water, and sharing funny, tender moments from the session.
- **Reflection:** Triadic sex is not a chaotic free-for-all; it requires choreography, patience, and deep listening.
- **Haven opportunities:** Triad focus rotation timer; Cuddle Mode quick signal; multi-person intimate agreements.

### Scenario 3: The Polycule Town Hall & Monthly Budget Review
- **Context:** A 5-person polycule sharing a rural farmhouse and collective expenses.
- **Dynamic:** Collective governance and communal resource sharing.
- **What they want:** Managing farm chores, shared vehicle maintenance, and grocery budgets with full transparency.
- **Before:** All 5 partners populate their income and expense items on Haven's Polycule Ledger.
- **Experience:** Sitting around the large farmhouse dining table on the first Saturday of the month with fresh pastries. They review utility bills, allocate garden chores, and resolve minor domestic frictions collaboratively.
- **Branches:** Decisions are made by consensus: if one partner feels uncomfortable with a major expense, it is discussed until a win-win path is found.
- **Aftercare:** Going outside to weed the garden together, listening to music and enjoying communal life.
- **Reflection:** Intentional polyamory can build resilient, sustainable modern community structures.
- **Haven opportunities:** Polycule collective ledger; consensus voting mechanism; communal chore allocation board.

### Scenario 4: The Swinging Couple + Third — First Hotel Suite Encounter
- **Context:** Established married couple inviting a single female partner for an erotic weekend.
- **Dynamic:** Couples-plus-one consensual casual exploration (Unicorn play done ethically).
- **What they want:** Ensuring the guest partner feels completely empowered, respected, and safe from 'unicorn hunting' tropes.
- **Before:** Having drinks earlier in the week; establishing that the guest holds absolute veto power over every act.
- **Experience:** In a luxury hotel suite with music and champagne, the evening unfolds at the guest's pace. Both primary partners prioritize her pleasure, constantly asking for verbal consent before escalating.
- **Branches:** If the guest indicates tiredness or wants to stop, the scene ends instantly with warmth and room service.
- **Aftercare:** Ordering late-night room service, laughing, and ensuring the guest has a safe, comfortable ride home or private room.
- **Reflection:** Ethical three-way play requires putting the guest's comfort above the couple's fantasy.
- **Haven opportunities:** Guest partner empowerment charter; multi-party verbal consent check-ins; hotel scene guidelines.

### Scenario 5: Managing Metamour Friction & The Neutral Mediator
- **Context:** Two metamours experiencing passive-aggressive friction over holiday scheduling.
- **Dynamic:** Conflict mediation within a polyamorous network.
- **What they want:** Resolving the tension without putting the shared hinge partner in the agonizing middle.
- **Before:** Agreeing to meet with a poly-informed relationship coach or neutral friend via video call.
- **Experience:** The mediator helps each metamour voice their real fear: Metamour A fears losing holiday traditions; Metamour B fears being treated as a disposable secondary. Naming the fears clears the hostility.
- **Branches:** They negotiate a creative shared schedule: Thanksgiving lunch with Metamour A, dinner and black Friday with Metamour B.
- **Aftercare:** The hinge partner weeps with relief and gratitude, hugging both partners.
- **Reflection:** Direct metamour communication prevents the hinge from suffering emotional burnout.
- **Haven opportunities:** Metamour conflict resolution worksheet; mediation agenda template; holiday compromise matrix.

### Scenario 6: The Triad Commitment Ceremony & Ring Exchange
- **Context:** Three partners celebrating their 5th anniversary with a private commitment ceremony.
- **Dynamic:** Non-traditional spiritual marriage / commitment celebration.
- **What they want:** Exchanging rings and vows in front of their closest chosen friends in a private garden.
- **Before:** Writing a tripartite set of covenants reflecting their non-hierarchical commitment.
- **Experience:** Under a flower arch, each partner reads vows to the other two, sliding interlocking three-band gold rings onto each other's fingers. Their chosen community cheers and showers them with flower petals.
- **Branches:** They acknowledge legal realities (lack of multi-person civil marriage) while celebrating their spiritual truth.
- **Aftercare:** A joyful garden feast with dancing, wine, and laughter under twinkling lights.
- **Reflection:** Love and commitment are validated by the hearts of the participants, not by state bureaucracy.
- **Haven opportunities:** Tripartite vow creation wizard; multi-ring exchange ceremony script; community celebration guestbook.

### Scenario 7: Polycule Childcare & The 'Aunt/Uncle' Dynamic Protocol
- **Context:** A polyamorous family with children navigating the role of non-biological partners.
- **Dynamic:** Intentional co-parenting and child safety boundaries.
- **What they want:** Providing clear, loving, consistent boundaries for children while honoring non-biological partners.
- **Before:** Establishing clear parenting roles: biological parents hold legal and disciplinary authority; metamours act as trusted loving 'Aunts/Uncles'.
- **Experience:** The children grow up surrounded by multiple trusted, vetted adults who help with homework, school pickups, and soccer games, experiencing an abundance of emotional security.
- **Branches:** If a relationship between two adults ends, the adults commit to maintaining stability and gentle presence for the children.
- **Aftercare:** Weekly family meetings with children to discuss feelings, school, and family projects.
- **Reflection:** It truly takes a village to raise a child, and an ethical polycule can be that village.
- **Haven opportunities:** Family co-parenting boundary agreement; school pickup and logistics permissions; family meeting agenda.
