### Scenario 1: Consensual Non-Consent (CNC) — The Safehouse Scenario
- **Context:** Couple with 5 years of profound trust exploring consensual ravishment fantasy.
- **Dynamic:** Consensual Non-Consent (CNC) with rigorous multi-tier safewords.
- **What they want:** The psychological thrill of complete surrender to an overwhelming force without actual danger.
- **Before:** Extensive pre-scene contract on Haven: defining the time window (between 8 PM and 10 PM), prohibited acts, and non-negotiable safewords: verbal 'Red' or dropping a metal teaspoon.
- **Experience:** While the partner is reading in bed, the other enters with sudden, quiet intensity, pinning wrists and initiating passionate, forceful intimacy. The partner acts out resistance, fully knowing the safety boundaries hold firm.
- **Branches:** If at any point the teaspoon drops or 'Red' is spoken, the scene halts on the exact millisecond with zero hesitation.
- **Aftercare:** Immediate transformation into tender care: wrapping in blankets, hot tea, holding each other, continuous verbal reassurance ('You are safe, I am here, you have all the power').
- **Reflection:** Both conduct a mandatory Haven 24-hour emotional debrief to process any unexpected emotional triggers.
- **Haven opportunities:** CNC multi-tier safety agreement builder; non-verbal object-drop safeword monitor; mandatory 24h psychological debrief form.

### Scenario 2: Primal Chase & Capture in the Woods
- **Context:** Couple spending a weekend at an isolated private country cabin.
- **Dynamic:** Primal Predator / Prey consensual outdoor chase.
- **What they want:** The primal adrenaline rush of fleeing through safe terrain and being caught and pinned by a fierce, loving partner.
- **Before:** Walking the trail in daylight to identify tripping hazards, roots, and boundaries (no going near the stream).
- **Experience:** At dusk, the 'prey' is given a 60-second head start. Heart pounding with wild adrenaline, they run through the wooded trail until the 'predator' tackles them onto a soft mossy knoll with growls and passionate kisses.
- **Branches:** If an ankle turns or footing slips, the chase ends instantly to assess physical safety.
- **Aftercare:** Brushing pine needles out of hair, a warm shower together, checking for ticks/scratches, and drinking hot cider by the woodstove.
- **Reflection:** They marvel at how tapping into primal animal instincts strips away civilized neuroses.
- **Haven opportunities:** Trail boundary GPS geofence; outdoor terrain safety checklist; primal aftercare guide.

### Scenario 6: Kidnapping & Trunk Extraction Adventure
- **Context:** High-experience thrill-seeking couple designing an immersive adventure scenario.
- **Dynamic:** High-production-value consensual kidnapping simulation.
- **What they want:** The heart-racing cinematic experience of an unexpected capture, transport, and rescue/interrogation.
- **Before:** Setting the 3-day activation window; confirming safety: no real public spaces where bystanders could call the police; designated safe location.
- **Experience:** While walking to their secluded garage at home, the partner is hooded, placed in the backseat of their own car, driven around the block, and brought into their darkened basement decorated as a safehouse.

- **Aftercare:** Hot soup, taking off all gear, looking at funny photos of the prep, and debriefing the adrenaline surge.
- **Reflection:** They recognize that elaborate scenarios require meticulous planning to keep everyone physically and legally safe.
- **Haven opportunities:** Kidnapping simulation planning checklist; bystander risk mitigation guide; adrenaline decompression protocol.

### Scenario 7: The Master-Class Interrogation — Secrets & Lies
- **Context:** Couple testing their ability to maintain poker faces and detect micro-expressions.
- **Dynamic:** Psychological interrogation game with sensual stakes.
- **What they want:** An intellectual, psychological battle of wits where catching a lie earns an erotic forfeit.
- **Before:** Writing down 5 true secrets and 5 plausible lies on cards.
- **Experience:** Under bright desk lighting, the interrogator asks probing questions while monitoring pulse with two fingers on the wrist. When a lie is detected, the subject must surrender an article of clothing or submit to a tickle or paddle forfeit.
- **Branches:** If the subject successfully fools the interrogator on 3 consecutive questions, the roles flip instantly.
- **Aftercare:** Sharing a drink, revealing the truth behind all the stories, and laughing about the deceptions.
- **Reflection:** They find that playful deception within a safe game enhances intimacy and verbal sparring.
- **Haven opportunities:** True/Lie card deck manager; pulse and tell tracker; forfeit selector wheel.
