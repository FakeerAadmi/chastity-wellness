## 2. Engine 1: Desires & Discovery Decks

The Desires Engine allows participants to discover mutual kinks, fantasies, and curiosities without vulnerability or fear of rejection.

### Double-Blind Matching Architecture
- **Blind Discovery:** Each participant independently rates cards from curated or custom desire decks.
- **Rating Tiers:**
  - `Eager / Love to Do`: Active desire; enthusiastic interest.
  - `Curious / Open to Try`: Willing to explore under safe, structured conditions.
  - `Fantasy Only`: Erotically exciting in thought, but participant does *not* want to enact in physical reality.
  - `Not for Me`: Neutral disinterest.
  - `Hard Limit`: Inviolable boundary; automatically writes to the participant's hard limits registry.
- **Reveal Mechanics:**
  - A fantasy is **only revealed as a match** if *both* partners rate it `Eager` or `Curious`.
  - If Partner A selects `Eager` and Partner B selects `Not for Me` or `Hard Limit`, **Partner B is never informed that Partner A selected it**, protecting Partner A from vulnerability or shame.
  - Matches are sorted into an "Exploration Queue" ready to be converted into Agreements or Sessions.

## 3. Engine 2: Permission Requests

In asymmetric dynamics (Power Exchange, Chastity, Hotwifing), authority is negotiated through formal requests.

### Request Lifecycle & Schema
- **Request Types:**
  - *Chastity Release Request:* Requesting cage removal for exercise, hygiene, intimacy, or medical relief.
  - *Outside Date Request:* Cuckold/Hotwife proposal to schedule an encounter with an outside partner.
  - *Purchase / Attire Request:* Submissive asking approval for clothing, lingerie, or toy purchases.
  - *Scene Proposal:* Proposing a specific BDSM scene date and parameters.
- **Workflow:**
  1. `Drafted`: Submitting participant specifies purpose, duration, and conditions.
  2. `Submitted`: Approving partner receives a discrete, ambient notification.
  3. `Decision`:
     - **Approved Unconditionally**
     - **Approved with Modification** (e.g., "Approved for 45 minutes; must send photo when relocked")
     - **Denied with Erotic / Dignified Rationale** (e.g., "Denied; your lockup continues until Friday evening")
     - **Negotiation Conference Requested**

## 4. Engine 3: Tasks & Proof Verification

The Task Engine manages assignments, service obligations, physical training, and erotic challenges.

### Structure & Verification
- **Task Attributes:** `id`, `title`, `description`, `assigneeId`, `assignedById`, `deadline`, `cadence` (`once`, `daily`, `weekly`), `proofType` (`none`, `text_journal`, `ephemeral_photo`, `timer_completion`, `sensor_metric`), `status` (`pending`, `submitted`, `verified`, `forfeit`).
- **Proof Workflows:**
  - *Ephemeral Photo Proof:* Submissive captures photo within the app; photo displays to Dominant with view-once or time-decay options; photo is **never saved to the device gallery or public cloud**.
  - *Timer Verification:* App tracks active focus time (e.g., 20 minutes kneeling, 30 minutes reading kink literature).
- **Grading & Feedback:** Dominant can mark tasks `Verified & Approved`, `Flawless Service`, or `Unsatisfactory / Forfeit Due`.

## 5. Engine 4: Rituals (Habituated Protocols)

Rituals are recurring, step-by-step practices that ground a dynamic in everyday life.

### The Step-by-Step Runner
- **Examples:** Morning Keyholder Inspection, Evening Debrief & Foot Washing, Daily Chastity Hygiene Protocol, Sunday Polyamory Schedule Calibration.
- **Execution Flow:**
  - Step 1: Physical Preparation (e.g., "Prepare tea; assume seated posture").
  - Step 2: Inspection / Check (e.g., "Inspect cage cleanliness and skin integrity").
  - Step 3: Affirmation / Devotion (e.g., "Read agreed dynamic affirmations").
  - Step 4: Completion Confirmation.
- **The Non-Punitive Pause:** At any point during a ritual, either participant can tap **"Pause / Stop"** to immediately exit the protocol without penalty if emotional or physical comfort is compromised.

## 7. Engine 6: Check-ins & Health Analytics

Check-ins track the health of relationships and dynamics over time, preventing silent resentment, subspace drop, or emotional burnout.

### Multi-Dimensional Scales
Haven measures three distinct calibration axes (1–10 scale):
1. **Emotional Wellbeing:** Overall mood, vulnerability, safety, and happiness.
2. **Consent & Agency:** Feeling heard, respected, and in complete control of boundaries.
3. **Dynamic Alignment:** Satisfaction with power balance, frequency of intimacy, and protocol adherence.

### Intelligent Pattern Detection
- **Drop Detection:** If a participant's Emotional score drops significantly within 48 hours of an intense Session, Haven flags a gentle notification: *"Subspace / Scene Drop alert: Consider initiating a gentle aftercare check-in with your partner."*
- **Agreement Friction Alert:** If Consent or Alignment scores trend below 6 for three consecutive check-ins, Haven prompts: *"Time for a scheduled Agreement Review."*

## 9. Engine 8: The Composable Experience Builder

One of Chaster's greatest innovations was its community extension architecture (wheel spins, dice rolls, verification tasks, lock extensions). However, Chaster limited this exclusively to male chastity hardware.

**Haven generalizes this into the Composable Experience Builder:** a modular, drag-and-drop or node-based framework that allows partners to design custom, dynamic erotic experiences for **any** relationship dynamic.

### Architectural Nodes of an Experience
An Experience is constructed as a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of interchangeable nodes:

```
[ TRIGGER ] ──► [ CONTEXT ] ──► [ DECISION BRANCH ] ──► [ ACTION / TIMER ] ──► [ RESOLUTION ]
```

1. **Trigger Nodes:**
   - *Time-Based:* Daily at 8:00 AM, Every Friday night, Countdown expiration.
   - *Event-Based:* Task completed, Request approved, Lock engaged, Check-in logged.
   - *Spontaneous:* Partner presses "Initiate Experience."
2. **Context & Requirement Nodes:**
   - Verify active agreement exists.
   - Verify participant readiness and absence of active red safeword.
3. **Decision & Probability Nodes:**
   - *Chance / Dice:* Randomized selection (e.g., "Roll 1d6: 1=Spanking, 2=Denial, 3=Edging, 4=Service, 5=Massage, 6=Free Choice").
   - *Asymmetric Choice:* Dominant chooses between Path A and Path B.
   - *Secret Choice:* Both partners submit a blind choice; if they match, a special reward triggers.
4. **Action & Hardware Nodes:**
   - Lock / Unlock Bluetooth device for X minutes.
   - Activate vibrator pattern on Lovense / We-Vibe toy.
   - Require photo proof before advancing.
   - Enforce 20-minute kneeling or stillness timer.
5. **Resolution & Feedback Nodes:**
   - Award dynamic favor points.
   - Log outcome to Shared Memory Vault.
   - Trigger mandatory Aftercare checklist.
